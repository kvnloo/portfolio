The decomposition is the product

The top-level z0 repository is deliberately not a runtime. It owns the map, installer, contract registry, and architecture documentation. Runtime behavior, experiments, and publication live elsewhere so an architectural label cannot silently become evidence that a mechanism works.

Architecture at a revision, not architecture as decoration

A repository can publish a zer0.repo.yaml describing implemented mechanisms, interfaces, representations, ownership boundaries, and evidence locations at that Git ref. The central registry owns canonical identities; implementation manifests cannot redefine them.

Implementation docs live with implementations. Architecture and onboarding live in z0.

Evaluation is outside the policy being evaluated

z0intelligence separates execution from evaluation. A bounded live policy produces actions and outcomes; replayable state, objective counters, reference semantic readings, and independent outcomes feed Evolution Lab. A candidate earns production traffic through measurement rather than because its generating model also judged it successful.

The current implementation is narrower than the roadmap

The public z0intelligence repository explicitly distinguishes what exists today from the longer-term personalization system. Today it contains the OpenJev runtime/benchmark substrate: runtime-defined typed options, generation-free direct-logit scoring, shared-state prefix reuse, trainable one-pass scorers, frozen evaluation bundles, and Evolution Lab integration. Personal-history onboarding and the larger “army of specialists” vision remain staged roadmap work.

A representative reported result

The repository currently reports an RTX 3090 benchmark where direct typed logits returned 21 probability pairs in a median 1.023 s versus 5.332 s for a compact autoregressive JSON-array baseline. The portfolio keeps this marked reported until the frozen result/method/reproduction artifacts are promoted into the canonical manifest at an exact revision.

Publication rules are intentionally strict

  • Every public post claim should resolve to a frozen study artifact.
  • Source-reported benchmark numbers and reproduced measurements are different fields.
  • Training/evaluation splits preserve work-item lineage.
  • The system generating a label cannot silently become its own verifier.
  • Negative and incomplete results are publishable; failed hypotheses are not hidden.
  • Raw secrets, personal traces, and private prompts do not enter the public evaluation repository.

Why this belongs in the portfolio

The strongest signal is not “many AI repos.” It is the repeated insistence on ownership boundaries, explicit contracts, frozen evidence, abstention, and promotion gates. The ecosystem is the case study; the individual repositories are components and receipts.
