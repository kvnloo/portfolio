Case study · Verified OSS Loop

Contribution quality over contribution volume.

The OSS portfolio is organized around inspectable contribution trails: what problem existed, what changed, what exact revision was tested, what failed, and what the upstream maintainer ultimately decided.

The protocol

Verified OSS Loop treats agent-assisted contribution as a controlled engineering process rather than a PR factory. Workers claim bounded work, operate on isolated revisions, attach evidence, and can request independent review. They do not get merge authority.

  • claim leases make ownership/expiry visible;
  • evidence is revision-bound instead of described vaguely;
  • failures and blocked attempts remain receipts rather than disappearing;
  • independent verification can check the exact head;
  • the upstream maintainer remains the final authority.

Example: a merged correctness proof in CUA

trycua/cua #4317 added a real two-session Driver/MCP/Chromium isolation proof. The test deliberately crossed browser targets and refs between sessions and required structured refusal before either wrong fixture could mutate. It then retired one session and proved the unrelated session remained usable.

The PR is explicit about scope: it proves the browser target/ref plus session-end portion of the isolation matrix; capture/native-token and cancellation ownership remain separate owners. That limitation is part of the evidence, not an inconvenient footnote.

Example: a small merged fix in Raspberry Pi tooling

raspberrypi/rpi-image-gen #334 declared two host dependencies the build already used directly—ca-certificates for HTTPS fetches and xz-utils for .tar.xz extraction—rather than relying on them arriving transitively on normal Debian installs.

This is deliberately shown beside the larger CUA proof. Useful OSS work ranges from deep behavioral evidence to a small dependency correction; the common standard is that the problem and outcome are inspectable.

Example: open is not merged

openmc-dev/openmc #4151 currently remains open. It consolidates duplicated weight-window test inputs onto canonical regression fixtures and reports passing format plus test/coverage checks at head 3f4a8c6f56b2d0742e4ac7c1e0134d2ebc981944. The portfolio should present it as reviewed/open evidence, not as shipped upstream work until the maintainer disposition changes.

What the OSS page should optimize for

Not “hundreds of PRs.” The useful unit is a contribution trail with upstream context, exact state, evidence, maintainer interaction, and an honest outcome. Aggregate counts can exist as secondary metadata once the ingestion corpus is fresh.

Merged, open, rejected, and experimental are different facts.
